Verify: Jest parser-to-chips tests, and the Maestro `capture` flow asserting the keyboard is up within 250 ms. Maestro cannot time the keyboard, so it was measured from screen recordings with touches shown (release build, Android 14 emulator, five taps on the add button): the keyboard starts to appear 80 to 200 ms after the touch (median 135 ms) and has finished the system's slide-in at 300 to 360 ms; the slide is the keyboard's own animation, so "up within 250 ms" holds for the keyboard appearing, not for it settled. The field wraps at large text sizes.
