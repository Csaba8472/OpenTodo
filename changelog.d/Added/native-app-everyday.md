- **A native OpenTodo app for Android and iPhone (in trial).** Five tabs (Today, Inbox, Upcoming, Browse, Search) drawn with the phone's own parts, swipe right to complete with Undo, swipe left to schedule or delete, a long-press menu, Select for several tasks at once and drag to reorder in a project, a capture sheet that reads dates, projects, labels and priority as you type, a task's detail edited in place with Markdown notes, the system's date picker, saved views, notes, search and the Logbook, all on the phone and offline. Sign-in goes through the phone's own sign-in sheet, and an encrypted account unlocks with its passphrase. See [The OpenTodo app](docs/app.md).
