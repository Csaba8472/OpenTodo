- **Signing in to the OpenTodo app reaches its confirmation every time.** The installed web app no longer answers the sign-in sheet's `/app/sign-in` itself, so the server's confirmation always opens; a new server's welcome note and start guide wait until it is answered instead of covering it, and on an encrypted account it opens over the passphrase screen; and after signing in with a password, a reload in Safari no longer leaves the sheet on the Inbox (the return address stays in the page until it leaves). Connecting an AI assistant through `/oauth/authorize` gets the same return.
